Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

Lightning Ledgerz, LLC ("Lightning Ledgerz," "we," "us") provides bookkeeping, financial planning & analysis, and fractional CFO services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, company name, and phone number when you create a portal account.
  • Financial data you share with us — documents you upload to your portal and, if you choose to connect QuickBooks or another accounting platform, the accounting data those platforms authorize us to read (for example your profit & loss, balance sheet, transactions, and customer revenue).
  • Payment information — subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Sign-in via Google — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, or files.

How we use it

  • To deliver the services you hire us for: bookkeeping, monthly financial reports, budgets, forecasts, and advisory work.
  • To operate your client portal, deliver reports to you, and respond to your requests.
  • To bill you for services you've agreed to.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the service: Supabase (secure database and authentication), Stripe (payments), Intuit QuickBooks (when you connect it), and email delivery providers for messages we send you. Each processes your data under its own security and privacy obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

Security & retention

Data is stored in access-controlled systems in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. Accounting-platform connections use OAuth — we never see your QuickBooks password, and you can revoke our access at any time from your Intuit account. We keep your data while you have an account with us; ask us to delete it and we will, subject to records we're legally required to keep.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account data by emailing us.
  • Disconnect QuickBooks at any time (Intuit account → Apps, or ask us).
  • Unsubscribe from marketing email with one reply; service emails (like report delivery) follow your account settings.
